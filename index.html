About

I'm an electrical engineer who likes working where hardware meets software. Most of my time goes into control systems and embedded work — getting sensors, motors, and microcontrollers to behave — with plenty of CAD and 3D printing along the way to turn ideas into parts I can put on a bench.

On the software side I reach for Python and JavaScript, live in Linux, and contribute to open source when I can. Outside of engineering work I tutor math and mentor new programmers, because the best way to really understand something is to explain it to someone else.
